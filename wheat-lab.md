{: .section-heading }

*The lab website is coming soon.*

Dr. Jiang is accepting undergraduate research assistants, welcoming inquiries from prospective graduate students, and recruiting for a funded, one-year postdoctoral position. She especially welcomes individuals interested in child development, caregiver well-being, family relationships, or the role of AI in parenting and mental health.
