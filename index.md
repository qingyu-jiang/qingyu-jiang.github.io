<h1 class="visually-hidden">About Qingyu Jiang</h1>
<div class="profile">
  {% include academic-portrait.html %}
  <div class="biography" markdown="1">

Qingyu Jiang is an Assistant Professor of Psychology in the Department of Psychology and Communication at the University of Idaho, where she directs the Well-being, Health, Early Adaptation and Thriving (WHEAT) Lab. She is also an affiliate faculty member with the Margaret Ritchie School of Family and Consumer Sciences and the Center of Biomedical Research Excellence in Nutrition and Women’s Health.

She received her Ph.D. in Human Sciences, with a specialization in Child, Youth, and Family Studies, from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln. She then completed a postdoctoral fellowship in maternal and child mental health at Brigham and Women’s Hospital/Harvard Medical School.
